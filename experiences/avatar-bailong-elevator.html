Avatar Mountains & Bailong Elevator

The 90-second lift to Pandora

Ride the world's tallest outdoor elevator straight up a cliff face to the Yuanjiajie plateau — the real-world landscape that inspired Avatar's floating Hallelujah Mountains.

At a glance

The Bailong Elevator is a 326 m glass outdoor lift built into a cliff in Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, China. It lifts visitors to the Yuanjiajie plateau in about 90 seconds, opening onto the quartz-sandstone pillars that inspired Avatar.

Why this experience matters

Yuanjiajie is the most famous postcard of Zhangjiajie, and the Bailong Elevator is the fastest way to reach it. The lift climbs 326 m up a sheer cliff — the world's tallest outdoor elevator — and drops you at the rim of the Avatar viewpoint loop.

For many travelers, this is the moment the landscape becomes real. The pillars that looked painted from below are suddenly beside you, wrapped in mist or glowing in sunrise. If you have only one morning in the park, this is the experience to prioritize.

Top highlights

The four must-sees once you step off the elevator.

Avatar Hallelujah Mountain

The Avatar viewpoint

The pillar that inspired the film's floating mountains. Best at sunrise before the crowds arrive.

First Bridge Under Heaven

Natural stone arch

A narrow stone bridge connecting two pillars, just a short walk from the elevator.

Enchanting Terrace

Wide panorama

A railed overlook with the classic sea-of-pillars view stretching into the distance.

The elevator itself

326 m in 90 seconds

The glass lift shows the cliff rushing past — dramatic, fast, and worth it for the view alone.

Recommended routes

Two ways to build this into your day.

Sunrise Avatar loop

2.5–3 hours · elevator up + shuttle/walk down

  1. 1

    Arrive before 07:00 enter at the East Gate and take the first shuttle to the elevator.

  2. 2

    Bailong Elevator up ride to the Yuanjiajie plateau.

  3. 3

    Avatar viewpoint walk to Hallelujah Mountain for sunrise photos.

  4. 4

    First Bridge Under Heaven short ridge walk to the natural arch.

  5. 5

    Descent return by elevator or walk down via the Golden Whip Stream path.

Yuanjiajie + Tianzi combo

Full day · shuttle between plateaus

  1. 1

    Bailong up early start at the elevator for the Avatar loop.

  2. 2

    Rim shuttle connect to the Tianzi Mountain ridge.

  3. 3

    Tianzi viewpoints Helong Deck and Imperial Brush Peak.

  4. 4

    Cable car down return to the valley by the Tianzi cable car.

Best time to experience

Sunrise

Arrive before 07:00 for the best light and smallest queues.

Apr – May

Spring mist and green forests.

Sep – Nov

Crisp air and clear visibility.

Avoid midday 09:00–11:00 when the elevator queues are longest. Summer afternoons can be hazy.

Travel tips

Beat the queues

The first 30 minutes after opening are the quietest. Arrive at the East Gate 30 minutes before it opens.

Ticket is separate

Park entry (¥227) does not include the elevator. Buy the elevator ticket separately, roughly ¥65 one way.

Best photo spot

The Avatar viewpoint is best at sunrise. The elevator top also has a small platform for a quick shot.

Crowds are real

If the elevator line is long, consider walking up via the Golden Whip Stream path — slower but scenic and quiet.

Clouds help

A little mist makes the pillars look like they are floating. Dry, sunny days can feel flat by comparison.

How to get there

City → East Gate Bus from Zhangjiajie city or railway station to the Wulingyuan East Gate, 40–60 min, ¥13.

East Gate → elevator Inside the park, take the shuttle to the Bailong Elevator station, ~10 min.

Elevator up Ride to the Yuanjiajie plateau in ~90 seconds.

Walking option From Golden Whip Stream, a stepped path climbs to Yuanjiajie in 1.5–2 hours.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does the Bailong Elevator take?

The ride takes roughly 88–90 seconds to climb 326 m. Most of the time is spent loading and unloading, so budget 5–10 minutes total during quiet periods and up to an hour in queues at peak times.

Is the Avatar Hallelujah Mountain the same as in the film?

The floating mountain in the film is CGI, but the real-world inspiration is the Southern Sky Pillar viewpoint at Yuanjiajie, now widely called Avatar Hallelujah Mountain. The pillars are real quartz-sandstone formations.

Do I need a separate ticket for the elevator?

Yes. The Wulingyuan combo ticket (about ¥227, valid 4 days) covers park entry and shuttle buses. The Bailong Elevator is roughly ¥65 one way or ¥130 round-trip and is purchased separately.

Can I walk up instead of taking the elevator?

Yes. A stepped path climbs from the Golden Whip Stream area to the Yuanjiajie plateau in about 1.5–2 hours. It is quieter and scenic, but steep in sections.

What is the best time to visit?

Sunrise is best for light and crowds. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–November) offer the most reliable mist and clear skies. Midday in summer is hazy and crowded.

How long should I plan for this experience?

Allow 2.5–4 hours: 30 minutes travel to the elevator, 90 seconds up, and 2–3 hours walking the Avatar viewpoint loop. Pair it with Tianzi Mountain for a full day.
