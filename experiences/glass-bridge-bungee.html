Glass Bridge & Bungee

Walk 430 m over the canyon — then jump off

The longest and highest glass-bottom bridge in the world, suspended 300 m above the Grand Canyon. Walk it, photograph it, or take the leap on one of the planet's highest bungee jumps.

At a glance

The Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge is a 430 m transparent glass-bottom bridge spanning the Grand Canyon, about 300 m above the valley floor. It also offers one of the world's highest bungee jumps for adrenaline seekers.

Why this experience matters

Part engineering marvel, part adrenaline test, the Grand Canyon Glass Bridge is impossible to ignore. The transparent deck lets you look straight down at the canyon 300 m below, and on quiet days the glass reflects the sky so completely it feels like walking on air.

For most visitors, the bridge is the main event. For a few, it is the warm-up. The bungee platform at the center of the bridge is one of the highest commercial jumps in the world — a 260 m free fall into the canyon.

Top highlights

What to expect once you step onto the glass.

The glass bridge

430 m transparent span

Walk the world's longest and highest glass-bottom bridge, suspended between two cliffs.

Canyon view

300 m straight down

The glass floor reveals the canyon floor far below — not for the faint of heart.

Bungee jump

One of the world's highest

A 260 m leap from the center of the bridge. Reservations and health checks required.

Canyon walk

Hike below the bridge

After crossing, hike down to the canyon floor, across waterfalls, and out by boat.

Recommended routes

Two ways to experience the bridge.

Bridge + canyon loop

3–4 hours · walk, hike, boat

  1. 1

    Shuttle to Grand Canyon from Wulingyuan or Zhangjiajie city.

  2. 2

    Cross the bridge walk the 430 m glass span with shoe covers.

  3. 3

    Hike down follow the canyon trail past waterfalls and cliffs.

  4. 4

    Boat exit a short boat ride takes you to the exit gate.

Bungee package

Half day · adrenaline focused

  1. 1

    Book ahead reserve the bungee slot online or through a guide.

  2. 2

    Health check weight and basic health screening at the platform.

  3. 3

    The jump 260 m free fall from the center of the bridge.

  4. 4

    Certificate + photos usually included in the package.

Best time to experience

Apr – Jun

Green canyon, mild weather.

Sep – Nov

Clear skies, best visibility.

Early morning

Smaller queues and cleaner glass.

Avoid rainy days — the glass gets slippery and the canyon floor hike can be muddy. Summer afternoons are crowded.

Travel tips

Book in advance

Glass Bridge tickets sell out on weekends and holidays. Book at least one day ahead.

Shoe covers required

You will put on cloth shoe covers before stepping onto the bridge. Wear easy-to-cover shoes.

No loose items

Drones, tripods, and sharp objects are not allowed on the bridge. Bags may be checked.

Bungee restrictions

Jumpers typically need to be 18–60 years old, within a weight range, and free of certain medical conditions.

Combine with nearby sights

The bridge is 30–40 minutes from Wulingyuan. Pair it with Baofeng Lake or Yellow Dragon Cave for a full day.

How to get there

Wulingyuan → Grand Canyon Shuttle or private car, about 30–40 minutes.

Zhangjiajie city → Grand Canyon Bus or private car, about 60 minutes.

Bridge entrance Walk from the parking area to the bridge entrance; bags are checked.

Exit Most visitors exit via the canyon trail and boat, returning to the same parking area.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How high is the Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge?

The bridge is about 300 m above the canyon floor and 430 m long, making it one of the highest and longest glass-bottom bridges in the world.

How much does the bungee jump cost?

The bungee jump from the center of the bridge typically costs ¥2,998–¥3,998 depending on season and package. Advance booking is strongly recommended.

Is the bridge safe?

The bridge is engineered to high safety standards and undergoes regular inspections. Glass panels are replaced as needed. Visitors must wear shoe covers and follow staff instructions.

Can I visit the bridge and the canyon in one trip?

Yes. The standard ticket includes both the bridge and a canyon hike below, ending with a short boat ride. Allow 3–4 hours.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable walking shoes and clothes suitable for hiking. The canyon trail can be slippery after rain. Avoid high heels or open-toe shoes.

How do I get there from Wulingyuan?

The bridge is about 30–40 minutes from Wulingyuan by shuttle or private car. Many tour packages include transport.
