Welcome to Zhangjiajie

Zhangjiajie (张家界) is a city in Hunan Province, central China, home to the Wulingyuan Scenic Area — a UNESCO World Heritage Site inscribed in 1992. The park contains over 3,000 quartz-sandstone pillars and peaks, many over 200 meters tall, rising from deep mist-carved valleys. This surreal landscape was the real-world inspiration for the floating "Hallelujah Mountains" in James Cameron's blockbuster film Avatar. If you've ever wondered "Where was Avatar filmed?" — this is it.

For international visitors, Zhangjiajie offers something no other destination can match: walk on the world's longest cable car ride up Tianmen Mountain (7.5 km), step onto the world's highest glass-bottom bridge at Grand Canyon (430 m above ground), hike through ancient forests along Golden Whip Stream where monkeys play at your feet, or take the world's tallest outdoor elevator (Bailong Elevator, 326 m) straight up a cliff face. Whether you're seeking adventure travel, photography, family vacations, or a once-in-a-lifetime nature escape, Zhangjiajie delivers.

Top Attractions in Zhangjiajie

Eight must-see destinations — the same iconic spots ranked in our Top 8 list, from the Avatar mountains to hidden caves.

Avatar · 阿凡达

Yuanjiajie (Avatar Mountains)

The real-life Pandora — sandstone pillars that inspired Avatar's Hallelujah Mountains. Ride the Bailong Elevator and walk the Avatar viewpoint.

Sea of Clouds

Tianzi Mountain

Legendary peak forest with rolling seas of clouds and crimson sunrises. Named after Xiang Dakun, the Tujia "Son of Heaven". Best at dawn.

World Record

Tianmen Mountain

Ride the world's longest cable car (7.5 km), climb the 999-step Heavenly Stairway, and walk the glass skywalk at 1,500 m.

Family Friendly

Golden Whip Stream

A flat 7.5 km valley walk along a crystal stream, shaded by peaks and home to playful wild macaques. Easy and perfect for families.

Thrill Seeker

Grand Canyon Glass Bridge

The world's highest & longest glass-bottom suspension bridge (430 m), 300 m above the canyon floor. Bungee jumping available!

Panorama

Huangshizhai

A classic loop trail with a 360° panorama of the quartz-sandstone peaks. "He who doesn't reach Huangshizhai hasn't seen Zhangjiajie."

Relaxing

Baofeng Lake

A pristine alpine lake surrounded by towering cliffs. Take a boat ride while local ethnic singers perform traditional folk songs. Pure serenity.

Underground Wonder

Yellow Dragon Cave

One of China's largest karst cave systems — 13 levels, underground rivers, stalactites and waterfalls spanning 48 hectares beneath the earth.
