Huangshizhai

黄石寨 · The View That Defines Zhangjiajie

A compact cliff-top loop with the parks most-quoted viewpoint — "He who has not been to Huangshizhai has not seen Zhangjiajie." A 360° ring of stone pillars with a cable car both ways.

At a glance

Huangshizhai (黄石寨) is a flat-topped sandstone mesa in the Wulingyuan core of Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, Hunan, China, about 1,080 m high. A circular cliff-edge trail gives a 360° panorama of the surrounding quartz-sandstone peaks, and a cable car makes it an easy half-day even for non-hikers.

Why Huangshizhai is a must-see

Huangshizhai is the classic "overview" stop. Unlike the long ridge walks, its a loop on a flat mesa top, so you get the full 360° ring of pillars without climbing all day. Locals say if you skip it, you havent really seen Zhangjiajie.

A cable car runs both ways from the East Gate area, so you can ride up, stroll the level loop, and ride down — perfect for travelers who want the view without the sweat. Theres also a stair path if you prefer to walk.

Because the loop is short and paved, its one of the best spots for a relaxed sunrise or sunset among the peaks. Give it a clear morning and the whole stone forest opens up below you.

Top highlights

Five stops on the circular cliff-top loop.

The Grand View Terrace

摘星台

The loops signature overlook — a 360° sweep of the quartz-sandstone forest. This is the "you havent seen Zhangjiajie" viewpoint.

Five-Finger Peak

五指峰

A cluster of five pillars said to look like a giant hand. One of the most recognizable silhouettes from the loop.

Huangshi Village

黄石寨村

The mesa-top clearing with tea stalls and the best place to rest and take in the silence between the peaks.

Sword Test Stone

定海神针

A slender lone pillar rising straight from the forest floor — a favorite subject for the parks postcard photographers.

Huangshi cable car

黄石寨索道

The cable car up and down the mesa, about 8 minutes each way, with great aerial views of the gorge.

Recommended routes

Two ways up and around — pick by energy.

Cable car loop

1.5–2 hours · easy, both ways by cable

  1. 1

    Cable car up from the East Gate area, ~8 minutes.

  2. 2

    Walk the loop the level cliff-top circle, ~1 hour.

  3. 3

    Grand View Terrace the 360° signature stop.

  4. 4

    Cable car down or descend the stone stairs.

Hike the stairs

2–3 hours · moderate, stair climb

  1. 1

    Climb the stair path about 4,000 steps to the mesa.

  2. 2

    Loop the rim clockwise for the best light.

  3. 3

    Cable car down to save your knees.

Best time to visit

Apr – May

Green peaks, comfortable, good light.

Sep – Nov

Crisp air, clear panoramas.

Sunrise

The 360° ring glows at first light.

Avoid: summer fog can flatten the view; the loop is exposed, so bring water and a hat. National Day (Oct 1–7) brings heavy cable-car queues.

Travel tips

Take the cable car

Both ways by cable car makes this an easy half-day with no serious climbing.

Go for clear mornings

The 360° view needs clear sky. Foggy days waste the panorama — save it for a blue morning.

Bring water and a hat

The loop is exposed with little shade. Carry at least 1 L per person.

Sunrise or sunset

The ring of pillars is most dramatic at low sun. Arrive at opening for the quietest light.

Pair with Golden Whip Stream

Do Huangshizhai in the morning, then walk Golden Whip Stream below in the afternoon.

How to get there

City → Wulingyuan East Gate. Bus from Zhangjiajie city or railway station, about 40–60 min, ¥13.

East Gate → Huangshizhai. Inside the park, take the shuttle to the Huangshizhai cable car station.

Cable car up. Ride to the mesa in about 8 minutes.

Loop and return. Walk the rim, then cable car or stairs down.

No metro or ride-hail inside the park — the official shuttle buses are the only way between gates. Keep your passport for ticket check.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where is Huangshizhai, and how do I get there?

Huangshizhai is in the Wulingyuan core of Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, about 40 km from the city. Take a bus to the East Gate (around ¥13, 40–60 min), then the park shuttle to the Huangshizhai cable car station.

Is Huangshizhai hard to climb?

Not if you take the cable car — it runs both ways, about 8 minutes each, so you can enjoy the 360° loop with almost no climbing. There is also a ~4,000-step stair path if you prefer to walk up.

Why is Huangshizhai famous?

Its the classic overview of Zhangjiajie — a flat mesa-top loop with a full 360° ring of sandstone pillars. The local saying goes: "He who has not been to Huangshizhai has not seen Zhangjiajie."

How long should I plan for Huangshizhai?

A half day is plenty: cable car up, about an hour around the level loop, cable car down. Pair it with Golden Whip Stream below for a full day.

What is the best time of year to visit?

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–November) give the clearest panoramas. Go on a clear morning — fog flattens the view, and the exposed loop gets hot in summer.

Do I need my passport and can I use cards?

Bring your passport for park tickets. Cards are unreliable at small vendors, so carry cash (RMB). Alipay works with a linked international card.
