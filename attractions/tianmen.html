Tianmen Mountain

天门山 · Heavenly Gate & Glass Skywalk

The mountain that looms over Zhangjiajie city itself — ride the worlds longest cable car, snake up the 99-bend road, walk through the natural Heavenly Gate arch, and test your nerves on a glass cliff walkway.

At a glance

Tianmen Mountain (天门山) is a standalone peak south of Zhangjiajie city, Hunan, China, about 1,518 m high. It is famous for the Heavenly Gate (天门洞), a 131 m natural arch, reached by the worlds longest passenger cable car and a 99-bend mountain road, with glass skywalks along the cliff edge.

Why Tianmen Mountain is a must-see

Tianmen is different from the Wulingyuan peaks — its a single dramatic mountain right next to the city, and the experience is engineered thrill as much as scenery. The cable car from downtown is the longest in the world at 7.5 km, climbing through clouds to the summit.

At the top, a walkway threads the cliff face, including sections of transparent glass floor. Then a path and stairs lead down to the Heavenly Gate, a vast natural arch pierced through the rock — the "Gate to Heaven" that gives the mountain its name.

Below, the 99-Bend Road coils up the mountainside like a dragon, and a escalator inside the cliff carries you the final stretch. Its the most "theme-park meets nature" day in Zhangjiajie, and easy to reach without entering the Wulingyuan park.

Top highlights

Five signature thrills of Tianmen Mountain.

Cable car up

天门山索道

The worlds longest passenger cable car at 7.5 km, rising from downtown Zhangjiajie through mist to the summit in about 30 minutes.

Heavenly Gate

天门洞

A 131 m natural arch punched through the cliff — the "Gate to Heaven". Reach it by the cliff escalator and a final stair climb.

Glass Skywalk

玻璃栈道

A cliff-edge walkway with a transparent glass floor, 1,430 m above the valley. Shoes covers provided; knees optional.

99-Bend Road

通天大道

The mountain road with 99 hairpin turns coiling up to the gate — a marvel of engineering and a dizzying view from above.

Summit loops

山顶环线

Paved cliff-top loops with lookouts over the city and the Yuanjiajie ranges beyond — calm after the adrenaline.

Recommended routes

Two ways to experience Tianmen — pick by nerves and time.

Half–full day · cable car up + glass walk + gate

    Cable car from downtown 7.5 km to the summit, ~30 min.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

  1. 1

    Hike the upper trails from the summit loops.

  2. 2

    Descend to the gate on foot via the stone steps.

  3. 3

    Return by bus the 99-Bend Road to the base.

Best time to visit

Apr – May

Green, comfortable, good visibility.

Sep – Nov

Crisp air, clear summit views.

After rain

Sea of clouds below the cable car.

Avoid: the glass walkway closes in heavy ice or storms; winter can be spectacular but slippery. National Day (Oct 1–7) means long cable-car queues.

Travel tips

Book the cable car early

Tianmen is hugely popular; arrive at opening to avoid the longest queue of your trip.

Check the glass walk status

It closes in ice or storms. If its closed, the cliff walkways and gate are still worth it.

Bring water and a layer

The summit is cooler and exposed. Carry at least 1 L per person.

Wear real shoes

You will climb many stairs to the Heavenly Gate. Skip sandals and heels.

Stay in the city

Tianmen is 15 min from downtown Zhangjiajie — no need to enter the Wulingyuan park.

How to get there

In the city. Tianmen Mountain is south of Zhangjiajie city center — the cable-car lower station is in town.

Cable car up from the downtown station, 7.5 km to the summit.

Or 99-Bend Road bus from the mountain foot if you skip the cable car.

Return downtown by cable car or road bus.

No metro or ride-hail inside the park — the official shuttle buses are the only way between gates. Keep your passport for ticket check.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where is Tianmen Mountain, and how do I get there?

Tianmen Mountain is a standalone peak south of Zhangjiajie city, Hunan. The cable-car lower station is in the city, so you can reach it by bus or taxi from the railway station in about 15 minutes — no need to enter the Wulingyuan park.

What is the Heavenly Gate?

The Heavenly Gate (天门洞) is a 131 m natural arch pierced through the cliff face. You reach it via a cliff-escalator and a final stair climb. It is the landmark that gives the mountain its name.

Is the glass skywalk safe?

Yes — the glass is reinforced and shoe covers are provided. It closes only in ice or storms. If closed, the cliff-edge walkways and the Heavenly Gate are still open.

How long should I plan for Tianmen Mountain?

A half to full day: cable car up (~30 min), summit loops and glass walk, down to the Heavenly Gate, then the 99-Bend Road bus to the base.

What is the best time of year to visit?

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–November) are best for clear views. After rain you may catch a sea of clouds below the cable car. Arrive early to beat the cable-car queue.

Do I need my passport and can I use cards?

Bring your passport for tickets. Cards are unreliable at small vendors, so carry cash (RMB). Alipay works with a linked international card.
