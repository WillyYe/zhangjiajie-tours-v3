Tianzi Mountain

天子山 · Sea of Clouds & Imperial Pillars

The highest and widest lookout over the Wulingyuan peaks — named for the Tujia "Son of Heaven" who led a revolt here. On a clear morning the stone pillars rise out of a rolling ocean of cloud.

At a glance

Tianzi Mountain (天子山) is the northwest ridge of the Wulingyuan Scenic Area in Zhangjiajie, Hunan, China, reaching about 1,262 m. It is the best place in the park to see the famous "sea of clouds" and the surreal quartz-sandstone pillars that gave Zhangjiajie its UNESCO fame.

Why Tianzi Mountain is a must-see

If Yuanjiajie is the postcard, Tianzi Mountain is the panorama. Sitting on the parks northwest rim at around 1,262 m, it offers the widest, highest vantage points over the quartz-sandstone forest — the same pillars that inspired the floating mountains in Avatar, seen from above rather than below.

The name means "Son of Heaven" (天子), after Xiang Dakun, a Tujia leader who rebelled against the Ming court here in the 14th century. Today the imperial metaphor lives on in viewpoint names like "Emperor Pen Peak" and "West Sea of Rocks".

What makes Tianzi special is the cloud. After rain the valleys fill with a silver sea of clouds and only the pillar tops poke through — a sight that pulls photographers up before dawn. Build in a buffer day; the peaks are shy when its foggy.

Top highlights

Five signature viewpoints along the Tianzi ridge — each a short shuttle or walk apart.

Helong Observation Deck

贺龙公园

The main Tianzi viewpoint and the highest overlook in Wulingyuan. On clear mornings the "sea of clouds" rolls beneath you and the imperial peaks stand like sentinels.

Imperial Brush Peak

御笔峰

A cluster of slender pillars said to look like the emperors writing brushes. It is one of the most photographed silhouettes in all of Zhangjiajie.

West Sea of Rocks

西海石林

A vast amphitheatre of stone peaks dropping into the mist. Walk the railing path at sunrise and the rock forest seems to float.

Fairy Scattered Flowers

仙女散花

A viewpoint where a lone peak appears to hold a basket of flowers — best lit in the soft gold of late afternoon.

Tianzi cable car up

天子山索道

A 2,000 m cable car that lifts you from the valley to the Tianzi ridge in about 20 minutes — the most scenic (and queueless) way up.

Recommended routes

Two ways to see Tianzi — pick by time, energy and weather.

  2. 2

  2. 2

Best time to visit

Apr – May

Spring green, comfortable, good cloud days.

Sep – Nov

Crisp air, clear skies, best visibility.

After rain

Mornings right after rain = sea of clouds.

Avoid: heavy summer fog can shroud the peaks for hours; National Day (Oct 1–7) and summer holidays mean long cable-car queues.

Travel tips

Ride the cable car, not the bus

The Tianzi cable car is the scenic way up and skips the long shuttle transfer from the East Gate.

Weather is everything

Peaks often vanish in fog. Build in a buffer day; mornings after rain give the best sea of clouds.

Bring water and snacks

Food on the ridge is limited and pricey. Carry at least 1 L water per person.

Arrive for sunrise

The sea of clouds is best 06:30–08:00. Stay near the East Gate and enter at opening.

Pair with Yuanjiajie

Tianzi and Yuanjiajie share the same plateau system — do them on one ticket across two mornings.

How to get there

City → Wulingyuan East Gate. From Zhangjiajie city or the railway station take a bus to the East Gate (武陵源东门), about 40–60 min, ¥13.

East Gate → Tianzi cable car. Inside the park, take the shuttle to the Tianzi Mountain cable car station (~40 min).

Cable car up. Ride to the Tianzi ridge in ~20 minutes, then follow the rim signs.

Alternative entry. You can also reach Tianzi via the Yuanjiajie plateau and the connecting shuttle.

No metro or ride-hail inside the park — the official shuttle buses are the only way between gates. Keep your passport for ticket check.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where is Tianzi Mountain, and how do I get there from Zhangjiajie city?

Tianzi Mountain sits in the Wulingyuan section of Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, about 40 km from the city. From Zhangjiajie city or the railway station take a bus to the parks East Gate (roughly 40–60 minutes, around ¥13), then the park shuttle to the Tianzi cable car, which lifts you to the ridge in about 20 minutes.

What is the "sea of clouds" and when can I see it?

After rain the valleys fill with a low layer of cloud and only the pillar tops show — the famous "sea of clouds". It is most reliable in spring and autumn, right after rainfall, typically 06:30–09:00. Clear, dry days show the pillars but no cloud sea.

Do I need a separate ticket for the Tianzi cable car?

Yes. Park entry (about ¥227, valid 4 days) covers the shuttle buses, but the Tianzi cable car is priced separately at roughly ¥72 one way or ¥144 round-trip. Many visitors ride up and shuttle or walk down.

How long should I plan for Tianzi Mountain?

A half day covers the main viewpoints from the Helong Deck along the West Sea of Rocks to Imperial Brush Peak. Pair it with Yuanjiajie on the same combo ticket and you will want a full day across two mornings.

What is the best time of year to visit Tianzi Mountain?

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–November) are best — mild weather and the clearest chance of the sea of clouds. Arrive early; overcast or foggy days can shroud the peaks, and cable-car queues peak mid-morning.

Can foreign tourists use credit cards, and do I need my passport?

Bring your passport — it is required to buy park tickets and may be checked at entrances. Cards are not reliable at small vendors inside the park, so carry some cash (RMB) for snacks, water, and the cable car. Alipay works if you have linked an international card.
