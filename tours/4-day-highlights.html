4-Day Zhangjiajie Highlights

Four relaxed days covering both Wulingyuan and the standalone Tianmen peak, plus a taste of local Tujia culture — the most complete first visit.

4 Days / 3 Nights From ¥2,680 / person

Overview

For travellers who want the full picture without racing, this four-day tour links the Avatar mountains and Golden Whip Stream, the Grand Canyon Glass Bridge, Tianmen Mountain, and a Tujia village visit for local food and handicrafts. Three nights based in Wulingyuan keep transfers short.

Day by day

A relaxed, guide-led rhythm — tell us if you want to slow down or speed up any day.

1

Day 1

Yuanjiajie & Tianzi

Bailong Elevator to Yuanjiajie Avatar loop, then Tianzi Mountain Sea of Clouds. Overnight Wulingyuan.

2

Day 2

Golden Whip Stream & Glass Bridge

Morning walk along Golden Whip Stream, afternoon at the Grand Canyon Glass Bridge (optional bungee). Overnight Wulingyuan.

3

Day 3

Tianmen Mountain

Transfer to Tianmen Mountain: cable car, cliff glass walk, Heaven’s Gate. Evening Tujia village feast. Overnight Wulingyuan.

4

Day 4

Village & departure

Morning Tujia village walk and market, then transfer to your next destination.

Frequently asked questions

Is 4 days enough for a first visit?

Yes — it covers the headline sights plus local culture with a relaxed pace. Most guests find it the sweet spot before adding Fenghuang or Fenghuang ancient town.

Can we add Fenghuang?

Easily as a 5th day. Fenghuang ancient town is about 2.5 hours from Zhangjiajie by car; we can extend the route.

What about the Tujia village?

A half-day visit with a wooden-village walk and a communal local feast. It is the most human, lowest-key part of the trip.

Are hotels flexible?

Yes. We default to clean standard hotels near Wulingyuan; upgrades (forest lodges, lake resorts) are available on request.
